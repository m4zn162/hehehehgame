💖 Jana vs. the Mochi Queen

Save Mazen!

A 3D boss-fight adventure. Fight through three dungeon rooms of mini Mochi kittens, then face the giant Mochi Queen with Jana’s pink blaster.

How to play

🖥️ Computer

WASD move · Mouse aim · Click shoot
Space jump · Shift dash

📱 Phone or tablet

Left stick moves, drag the right side of the screen to aim, and tap FIRE, JUMP and DASH. Landscape works best.

Meet the cast

👧 Jana

Brave hero with a pink blaster and long straight hair.

🧒 Mazen

Kidnapped and waiting in a golden cage. He needs a rescue (and a hug).

🐱 Queen Mochi

A giant crowned cat boss with orb volleys, spin sprays and shockwave slams.

😺 Mini Mochis

Chasers, orb-shooting kittens and a crowned captain.

The dungeon

Clear 3 rooms of kittens (each room is tougher and heals you a little), then beat the Mochi Queen to free Mazen.
